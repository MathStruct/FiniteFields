ADR 0005 Rank encoding of GF(2^n) in Conway's On2

ADR-0005 Rank encoding of GF(2^n) in Conway's On2

Nov 21, 20263 min read

  • design
  • characteristic-2
  • categorical

design characteristic-2 categorical

Status: accepted (2026-11-21, maintainer: “Approve ADR-0005 and R1”). Every field F2n​ is represented canonically as the subfield of Conway’s On2​ (the nimbers on all ordinals) with elements, each element encoded by its rank in ordinal order within that subfield. For this is exactly the existing Nimber type. Supersedes the part of ADR-0004 Nimbers as the default representation of GF(2^n) that made Conway polynomials the default for other .

Research: Canonical extension of the nimbers. Backlog: R1 (the data), then a library type.

Context

ADR-0004 made nimbers the default for and Conway polynomials the default otherwise. J1a implemented Conway fields and showed the costs: Lübeck’s table has gaps (no ), pseudo-Conway polynomials would need a non-canonical construction, and the isomorphisms between nimber and Conway fields needed a root convention chosen by us. The maintainer asked for a structure that extends the nimbers to every without arbitrary choices, even reproducible ones (StandardFF was rejected for its pseudo-random polynomial searches).

Options considered

  1. Conway polynomials (ADR-0004): canonical by a lexicographic rule, but tabulated, with gaps, not extending the nimbers (a separate representation joined by a chosen isomorphism).
  2. StandardFF (Lübeck 2023): extends the nimbers exactly on and covers every , but picks the odd-prime steps by deterministic pseudo-random searches. Rejected.
  3. Conway’s On2​ below ωωω with the rank encoding: every choice is forced by the mex definition of nim-multiplication (the same definition that gives the finite nimbers); every finite field is a subfield, embeddings are inclusions; numbering the elements of F2n​ by ordinal rank is F2​-linear and gives bits.

Decision

Option 3. The element of F2n​ with rank is the -th smallest ordinal (from 0) among the elements of the subfield of On2​ of that order; it is stored as the integer in the smallest fitting unsigned type and printed in hexadecimal, as nimbers are.

Consequences

  • Nimber is unchanged: for the subfield is the initial segment, rank = value.
  • Embeddings F2m​↪F2n​ (m∣n) are canonical (the inclusions in On2​), compatible, and fixed bit matrices in rank coordinates; equality across fields can be defined through them.
  • The runtime needs, per , a change of basis between rank coordinates and an internal basis for fast products (any basis: it does not leak), and the embedding matrices. These are computed once (R1) from On2​, which needs Lenstra’s elements αu​ for every prime u≤128; 17 of them were not known before R1.
  • Conway fields (J1a), AES and GHASH remain as alternative representations; embed connects them to the default through the canonical embeddings, which replaces J1a’s root convention.
  • The data are derived, so they are checked: against Lenstra’s table, against the nimbers for , by the field axioms, and by the compatibility of all embeddings.
