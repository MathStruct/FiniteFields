## V — Vault content

- **V0 — Restructure the vault.** Folders by subject (proposal: `Fields/`, `Characteristic 2/`,
  `Prime Fields/`, `Extension Fields/`, `Linear Algebra/`, `Analysis/`, `Category Theory/`,
  `Comparisons/`, `Algorithms/`), a `Map of Content.md`, a concept-note template in
  `vault/templates/`, by-name wikilinks.
- **V1 — Foundations.** One note each: field; characteristic; prime field; $\mathbb{F}_p$ as
  `ZMod p`; finite field; order is a prime power; existence and uniqueness of $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$
  (`GaloisField`); multiplicative group is cyclic; primitive element; Frobenius; subfield lattice
  ↔ divisibility of $n$; Galois group is cyclic generated by Frobenius; trace and norm; minimal
  polynomial; irreducible polynomials and counting them; Conway polynomials.
- **V2 — Characteristic 2 (priority).** $\mathbb{F}_2$ and Boolean arithmetic; $-1 = 1$ and
  addition is XOR; polynomial basis of $\mathbb{F}_{2^n}$; every element is a square and the
  square-root map; Artin–Schreier equation $x^2 + x = c$ and the trace criterion; normal bases;
  tower fields; quadratic forms in characteristic 2 (symmetric ≠ alternating, Arf invariant);
  standard fields $\mathbb{F}_{2^8}$ (AES), $\mathbb{F}_{2^{128}}$ (GHASH); examples worked by hand.
- **V3 — Categorical view.** Fields have no products and no initial object; the category of finite
  fields of characteristic $p$ and embeddings (hom-sets nonempty iff $m \mid n$, then of size $m$);
  $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_p$ as a colimit; absolute Galois group $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$; finite étale
  algebras ↔ finite continuous $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$-sets (Galois categories,
  `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Galois/`); Frobenius as a natural transformation; `ModuleCat` over
  $\mathbb{F}_q$.
- **V4 — Comparisons with $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$.** No ordering; only the trivial absolute
  value; discrete topology and no limits; **scalar products**: no `InnerProductSpace`, but
  bilinear/hermitian forms, isotropic vectors, failure of Gram–Schmidt, orthogonal complements
  that meet the space; **differentiability**: formal derivative, $(x^p)' = 0$, Hasse derivatives
  and Taylor expansion in characteristic $p$; Fourier analysis via additive characters
  (Walsh–Hadamard in characteristic 2); what "analysis over $\mathbb{F}_q$" means (scope of FF-004).
- **V6 — Nimbers and Conway polynomials:** nim-addition and nim-multiplication; the nimber fields
  $\mathbb{F}_{2^{2^k}}$ and the quadratic closure; Conway polynomials and their compatibility as a
  functor on the divisibility poset; pseudo-Conway polynomials; changes of representation as
  linear maps. Lean: CombinatorialGames' `Nimber` (check what it proves before relying on it).
- **V7 — Learners over finite fields** (the maintainer's experiment): what replaces gradients
  (discrete optimisation, interpolation, Reed–Muller, learning parity with noise), once the
  maintainer has written the ideas down.
- **V5 — Linear algebra over $\mathbb{F}_q$** (was FF-003): counting subspaces, $GL_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$
  order, rank, solving; specification before code.

## T — Tooling and site

- **T1 — Snippet sync tool.** Takes a declaration name, asks Lean (against the pinned mathlib)
  for signature, docstring and module, and writes a `> [!mathlib]` callout between markers
  (`%% mathlib: Name %%` … `%% /mathlib %%`) with a link to the mathlib docs and an attribution line
  (mathlib is Apache-2.0). Plain Markdown, so Obsidian and Quartz both render it; a re-run refreshes
  it; a renamed declaration fails loudly. The same tool syncs our own Lean and Julia snippets from
  anchors in the sources. Caveat: the hosted mathlib docs follow `master`, not our pin. A Quartz
  plugin would not render in Obsidian, hence the generator.
- **T2 — Lean API docs with doc-gen4** (the tool behind mathlib's docs), published under a subpath
  (e.g. `<site>/lean/`) beside the vault, as Lenticulum.jl publishes Documenter under `api/`;
  Julia API docs (Documenter) likewise under `<site>/julia/`. First check whether doc-gen4 can be
  limited to our modules: by default it documents every import, i.e. much of mathlib.
- **T3 — Note checker** (Lenticulum's `check_notes.py` as model): dangling wikilinks, missing tag
  lines, missing sources, unlisted `sorry`s.
- **T4 — CI, remaining:** a benchmark job (on demand, not per push) once `bench/` exists; a job
  that fails on an unlisted `sorry` (with T3).
- **T7 — Register `FiniteFieldArithmetic.jl`** in the General registry
  (`subdir=julia/FiniteFieldArithmetic.jl`) once it exists and has a first release.

## J — Julia (`FiniteFieldArithmetic.jl`)

- **J10 — Rank-encoded fields, remaining** (approved 2026-11-21; the type is done, see *Done*):
  faster `minimal_degree`/`hash` (Frobenius-power byte tables per maximal divisor; now 1.7 µs at
  $n = 126$); bulk operations (`MulBy`, matrix products, bitsliced vectors) for `Nim`, converting
  once at the boundary; `embed` between `Nim` and the Conway fields (now possible canonically:
  the Conway generator is an element of $\mathbf{On}_2$).
- **J1a — Representations, remaining** (approved 2026-11-21; first part done, see *Done*):
  nimbers for $n = 2^k$, Conway polynomials for every $n$ (pseudo-Conway beyond the tables),
  standard polynomials (AES, GHASH, ...); selected by a type parameter; conversion maps between
  them. **Decided (2026-11-19):** nimbers default, Conway secondary, the others alternatives.
  Printing always hex; the REPL adds the field when it does not fill its storage. Open:
  - **pseudo-Conway polynomials** for the degrees missing from Lübeck's table ($n = 128$ and 14
    others below it), compatible with the table; not canonical, so the construction needs the
    maintainer's choice;
  - **isomorphisms between AES/GHASH and the Conway fields** (there are $n$; which one, or all);
  - **Conway → Conway embeddings by byte tables** per pair (now 335 ns – 1.5 µs per element,
    the nimber isomorphisms 0.2–1.9 ns);
  - **table strategies** for small Conway fields (products 2.9 ns against 0.4 for nimber
    GF(256); inverses 14 ns with Itoh–Tsujii against 0.3);
  - bulk operations (`MulBy`, matrix products) for `PolyElement`;
  - whether Conway fields of different degrees should promote (and compare equal) through
    `embed`, as nimbers do; that needs `hash` through the smallest subfield (J9b).
- **J1b — Arbitrary $n$:** a function (or macro, if a function cannot give type stability) that
  constructs $\mathbb{F}_{2^n}$ for any $n$: picks the smallest fitting `UInt` for $n \le 128$,
  multi-word storage above; finds an irreducible polynomial (low-weight trinomial or pentanomial
  search with an irreducibility test) or takes a Conway polynomial from a table.
- **J2 — Multiplication strategies**, selected by dispatch and compared by benchmark; both table
  and computed variants where possible (maintainer, 2026-11-19), small fields probably tables
  only: shift-and-add, log/antilog tables (small $n$), carry-less multiplication (x86 `PCLMULQDQ`, ARM `PMULL` via LLVM
  intrinsics), reduction for sparse polynomials; profiled.
- **J3 — Inversion and division, remaining** (approved 2026-11-21; strategies done, see
  *Done*): division without a separate inverse (Euclid started at $s_1 = x$ gives $x/y$
  directly; `/` is still `x * inv(y)`); a faster Euclid (branch-free, or several quotient bits
  per step); bulk inversion (Montgomery's trick: $3(k-1)$ products and one inverse for $k$
  elements).
- **J4 — $\mathbb{F}_p$** in the smallest fitting `UInt`; Barrett/Montgomery as options.
- **J5 — $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ generic.**
- **J6 — Constant time:** not a goal. Document which strategies happen to be constant-time; do not
  engineer for it.
- **J8b — Bulk, remaining** (after J8; approved 2026-11-20; packing, the cache-blocking
  measurement, the bitsliced matrix product and bitsliced vectors at every level done, see
  *Done*): ARM `PMULL`; GPU kernels (needs a GPU to test).
- **J8f — Fewer carry-less products in `CLMulMatMul`** (proposed 2026-11-20, not approved): the
  matrix product is bound by `PCLMULQDQ` throughput, not by the caches (`src/bulk.md`, "Larger
  sizes and cache blocking"). Two terms per 64-bit product at GF(2^16) ($a_1 + a_2x^{32}$ times
  $b_2 + b_1x^{32}$); `VPCLMULQDQ` (four products per AVX-512 instruction) at every level.
  Tests and benchmark first, as usual.
- **J7 — $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_2$ ("$n = \infty$"):** every element lives in some finite
  $\mathbb{F}_{2^n}$, so elements carry their degree and an operation embeds both operands into
  $\mathbb{F}_{2^{\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)}}$ through Conway polynomials (pseudo-Conway beyond the
  tables). Exact, not fast; sizes grow with the lcm. The nimber quadratic closure (all
  $2^{2^k}$) is the cheap special case. After J1–J3. Lean counterpart:
  `AlgebraicClosure (ZMod 2)`.
- **J9b — Smallest field for the Conway representation and the others** (requested by the
  maintainer 2026-11-20, to be implemented with or after J1a/J1b; $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_2$ after
  J7; the nimber part, J9, is done): for an element `x` of a Conway field $\mathbb{F}_{2^k}$ (any
  $k$, not only powers of two), the caller can ask for either
  - the **integer** `minimal_degree(x)` $= d = [\mathbb{F}_2(x) : \mathbb{F}_2]$, the smallest
    $d \mid k$ with $x^{2^d} = x$ (type-stable), or
  - the **smallest containing field type**: `reduce_field(x)`, `x` as an element of the Conway
    field $\mathbb{F}_{2^d}$ (its type depends on the value, so type-unstable as for nimbers),
    through the Conway compatibility: $x$ is written in powers of
    $\beta = \alpha_k^{(2^k-1)/(2^d-1)}$, which the compatibility of Conway polynomials identifies
    with the generator $\alpha_d$ of $\mathbb{F}_{2^d}$.

  `minimal_level` has no Conway counterpart unless $k$ is a power of two. The divisors of $k$ can
  be tried from the smallest (or the largest proper ones ruled out first); measure which.
  Tests first, with the Frobenius oracle of `test/subfield.jl`; for $k = 2^j$, also cross-checked
  against the nimber results through the isomorphism of J1a.

## Done

- **R3 — Tower digits = binary digits, DiMuro's Lemma 1.1** (approved and done 2026-11-21):
  `lean/FiniteFields/NimberTower.lean`, no `sorry`. Under `IsTower` (each generator ${*}2^{a_j}$ a
  ring with `X ^ u_j ≤ leastNoRoots`, above the tower below) a tower monomial is one binary digit
  (`mono_eq`, via CombinatorialGames' `IsRing.pow_mul_eq`), the exponent is strictly increasing in
  the lexicographic tower-digit order, and echelon bases computed with tower digits give an
  order-preserving rank (`elem_lt_of_rank_lt`). Not part of R3 (not approved): proving `IsTower`
  for the concrete generators (DiMuro Thm 3.1, Lenstra's $\alpha_u$, `leastNoRoots_omega0`).
- **J10 — Rank-encoded $\mathbb{F}_{2^n}$ for every $n \le 128$** (approved and done 2026-11-21):
  `nim_field(n)` (`Nimber` for $2^k$, `Nim{n, T}` otherwise), products via the internal polynomial
  basis, `embed` by the inclusions of $\mathbf{On}_2$, `==`/`hash`/promotion across all degrees,
  `minimal_degree`/`reduce_field` (J9b for the default). Products 3.6–58 ns. Details in
  `src/nimfield.md`.
- **R2 — The rank encoding is order-preserving** (approved and done 2026-11-21): proof written out
  ([[Rank encoding is order-preserving]]); formalized in Lean without `sorry`, on
  CombinatorialGames' `Nimber` (`lean/FiniteFields/RankEncoding.lean`,
  `lean/FiniteFields/NimberDigits.lean`, `nimSum_lt_of_rank_lt`), with binary digits; the link to
  the tower digits of R1 is R3.
- **R1 — Canonical extension of the nimbers, step 2** (approved and done 2026-11-21): research
  implementation of Conway's $\mathbf{On}_2$ below $\omega^{\omega^\omega}$

- **J3 — Inversion strategies** (approved and done 2026-11-21): `InvStrategy` with `Fermat()`,

- **J1a, first part — polynomial-basis and Conway fields** (approved 2026-11-21, done

  `UInt`), `poly_field(n, low)` (Rabin's test), `conway_field(n)` for the 113 degrees $\le 128$
  of Lübeck's table (`src/conway_table.jl`; all entries checked irreducible and compatible,
  primitive up to 64, least up to 16), `GF256_AES`, `GF2_128_GHASH`; products by carry-less
  multiplication and Barrett reduction (2.5–3 ns up to degree 64, 9 ns above), inverses by
  extended Euclid (Fermat at 128); `embed` between Conway fields and, for $2^k \le 64$, between
  Conway and nimber fields, by a compatible choice of roots (the smaller of two at each level,
  to be confirmed by the maintainer). Details and measurements in `src/polyfield.md`.
- **J9 — Smallest field of an element, nimbers** (approved and done 2026-11-20):
  `minimal_level(x)`, `minimal_degree(x)` $= [\mathbb{F}_2(x) : \mathbb{F}_2]$ and
  `reduce_field(x)`, from the bit length (every subfield of $\mathbb{F}_{2^{2^K}}$ is a nimber

  `--enable-unswitch-cost-multiplier=false`. The function barrier stays. `bench/constmul_inline.jl`;
  `src/bulk.md`.
